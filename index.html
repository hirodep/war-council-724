3. Core strategy: One Arm Warrior

Each account goes deep in ONE faction, because power multiplies rather than adds:

Power = Troops × Research × Hero passives × Gear × Faction bonus

Build order per account:

  1. Faction camp: max it at each Watchtower level (unlocks higher troop tiers).
  2. Faction research: ATK, then DMG, then DEF, then HP.
  3. Faction heroes: level, star up, unlock passives. Highest-power heroes in Formation 1.
  4. Efficient training tech before mass training.

Milestone: T8 troops unlock at Watchtower 22–24. Other faction camps get only the minimum needed for Watchtower requirements.
